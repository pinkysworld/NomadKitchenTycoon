Expansion

Take the empire to the coast

Earn your reputation, bank the cash, and buy your way into Costa Verde — eight new districts from a working fishing harbour to a festival square that never quite closes. You keep everything you built; the map simply gets bigger.

City Map

Tap the pin, take the corner

Both cities are drawn as real illustrated maps. Your trucks show up where they are parked, the day's recommended route is marked, and every district is one tap away instead of buried in a list.

Recipe Book

A collection, not an inventory

Thirteen curated recipes unlock across the campaign, gated on reputation and District Mastery — so mastering the waterfront is what finally puts a lobster roll on your board.

Work the Line

Cook ahead, not just fast

A free grill station can now start the next customer's order while you plate the current one. Kitchen upgrades finally buy throughput you can feel, and a busy queue rewards planning over panic.

Fair Fights

Rebalanced from the first shift

Rush pacing was rebuilt around how service actually works, so a full queue is a challenge rather than a lost cause — and the guided first shift can no longer be failed.

Pricing

Budget, standard, premium, happy hour

Set a pricing strategy per truck and trade margin against footfall. Premium suits the financial district; happy hour fills a quiet promenade.
