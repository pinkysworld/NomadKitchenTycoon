Quick Help

Most common support topics

Restore purchases

Reopen the in-game store and use the restore purchases option. Apple handles payment processing, and the app refreshes unlocked items from your App Store account.

Remove ads

Ad-free access is available through the app's premium or remove-ads purchases. Once restored, banners, interstitials, and app-open ads should stop appearing.

Manage notifications

Open the in-game settings screen to turn reminders on or off. You can also change notification permissions in the iOS Settings app.

Reset saved progress

The app includes a reset-game-data option in Settings if you want to start from Day 1 again. This clears local save data on the device.

FAQ

Player support and review-ready answers

How do I report a bug or balancing issue?

Send an email to mip@gmx.biz with the issue, what you were doing before it happened, your device model, and the iOS version you are using.

Why am I still seeing ads?

Make sure your purchase is restored on the same Apple account used for the original transaction. If ads still appear after restoring, contact support and include the product you purchased.

Does the game require an account to play?

No in-app account creation is required. Game progress is stored on the device, and purchases are managed through Apple's App Store.

How do I turn off personalized advertising?

You can decline tracking permission when prompted by iOS, and you can review or change that choice later in Settings under Tracking. The game may still show contextual ads.

How do I delete my data?

Use the in-game reset option to remove local progress from the device. For questions about analytics and advertising-related data handled by third-party services, contact us and review the Privacy Policy.
