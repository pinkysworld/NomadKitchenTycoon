Privacy Policy

Clear, review-friendly privacy information for players and App Store reviewers.

Last updated: March 8, 2026. This policy explains what information Nomad Kitchen Tycoon stores locally, what third-party services may collect, and the controls available to players.

Contact

Privacy questions

mip@gmx.biz

Contact us if you need help understanding the app's privacy practices or want support related to your data and permissions.

1. Data stored on your device

The game stores progress and gameplay state locally on your device. This can include items such as unlocked trucks, recipes, staff progress, settings, analytics counters used inside the app, and local offer state tied to purchases.

2. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The app receives transaction and entitlement status needed to unlock paid content, restore purchases, and remove ads where applicable.

3. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app can schedule local reminders such as daily challenge resets, reward reminders, and win-back alerts. You can disable these in the app or in iOS Settings at any time.

Third-Party Services

Advertising, analytics, and consent technologies used by the app

Google Mobile Ads

The app uses Google Mobile Ads to show banner, interstitial, rewarded, and app-open ads. Depending on permissions and configuration, this may involve identifiers, advertising data, product interaction, diagnostics, and coarse location signals for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.

Google User Messaging Platform

The app uses Google's consent flow to present privacy and advertising choices where required. This may involve product interaction, performance data, and approximate location-related signals to determine consent requirements and messaging behavior.

Firebase Analytics

The app logs gameplay and monetization events such as session starts, rush completions, ad impressions, and purchases. These analytics help understand app usage, balancing, engagement, and feature performance.

Data Categories

Information that may be processed

Identifiers
Device identifiers may be processed for advertising, analytics, attribution, and fraud prevention.
Usage Data
Gameplay events, ad interactions, purchase-related actions, and other product interaction signals may be processed.
Diagnostics
Performance and diagnostic data may be processed to improve reliability, advertising systems, and analytics quality.
Approximate Location
Coarse location-related information may be used by integrated advertising and consent technologies.
Purchase History
The app may process purchase entitlements and product identifiers needed to unlock content and restore purchases.

Tracking And Choice

How advertising permissions work

App Tracking Transparency

On supported devices, the app may request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If permission is denied, the app may still show contextual advertising that does not rely on cross-app tracking permission.

Notification controls

Notification permissions can be changed at any time in iOS Settings, and in-game toggles are available for reminders and re-engagement alerts.

Resetting local data

The app includes a reset-game-data action in Settings. This removes local progress stored on the device and creates a fresh start.

No Account Required

What the app does not require from players

Nomad Kitchen Tycoon does not require players to create an in-app account with a name, username, or password in order to play.

The app does not collect payment card details directly. Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store.

If you contact support by email, we will receive the information you include in your message so we can respond to your request.
