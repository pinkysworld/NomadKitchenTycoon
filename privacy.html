Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Here's how Nomad Kitchen Tycoon handles your data.

Last updated: November 4, 2026. This policy applies to the iOS app (App Store) and the Android app (Google Play) and explains what information the app stores locally, what third-party services may collect through advertising and analytics, and the controls available to you.

Local Data

Information stored on your device

Game progress

All gameplay data — unlocked trucks, recipes, staff progress, currency balances, settings, and achievements — is stored locally on your device. On iOS, this data never leaves your phone unless you choose to back it up via iCloud or iTunes. On Android, the app allows Android's Auto Backup, so your device may back up the save file to your Google Account (for example Google Drive) according to your Android backup settings; you can turn this off in Android Settings.

In-app purchases

On iOS, purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. On Android, purchases are processed entirely by Google through Google Play Billing. In both cases the app receives only transaction status and entitlement information (such as the product ID, purchase state and purchase token) needed to unlock paid content, restore purchases, and remove ads where applicable. On Android, the app checks the digital signature of a purchase on your device before it unlocks anything.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app may schedule local reminders for daily challenges, reward availability, and gameplay events. You can disable notifications in the app settings or in iOS Settings at any time. The Android app does not schedule notifications.

Advertising & Analytics

Third-party services used by the app

Google AdMob

The app displays ads through Google AdMob. The iOS app uses banner, interstitial, rewarded video, and app-open ad formats; the Android app uses banner, interstitial (including one shown when the app opens) and rewarded video ads. Ad content is limited to the rating "G" (general audiences). Depending on your consent and device settings, AdMob may process device identifiers, advertising IDs (the Apple advertising identifier on iOS, the Android advertising ID on Android), IP address, interaction data, diagnostic information, and coarse location signals for ad delivery, measurement, frequency capping, and fraud prevention.

Google Privacy Policy

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

Before showing personalized ads, the app uses Google's UMP consent SDK to present a privacy and advertising consent dialog where required (e.g., in the EU/EEA and the UK) on both iOS and Android. On Android, no ad is requested before the consent status is known, and where a privacy options form is required you can reopen it from the app's settings. This ensures you can choose whether to allow personalized advertising or opt for non-personalized ads only.

Firebase Analytics (iOS only)

The iOS app uses Firebase Analytics to log gameplay events such as session starts, level completions, ad impressions, and purchase events. This data helps improve game balance, understand feature usage, and optimize the player experience. Firebase may process device identifiers and interaction data for these purposes. The Android app does not use Firebase at all: it contains no Firebase Analytics, no Firebase Crashlytics and no other Firebase component, and sends no data to Firebase.

Data Categories

Types of information that may be processed

Identifiers
Device identifiers and advertising IDs (Apple advertising identifier on iOS, Android advertising ID on Android) may be used for ad delivery, attribution, and fraud prevention, and on iOS for analytics.
Usage Data
On iOS, gameplay events, ad interactions, purchase events, session data, and other product interaction signals may be logged. The Android app does not log gameplay analytics.
Diagnostics
On iOS, crash and diagnostic data may be collected to improve app stability. The Android app does not collect crash reports; Google Play may provide us with aggregated crash data from users who opted in on their device.
Approximate Location
Coarse location data (e.g., country or region) may be used by ad networks for relevant ad delivery and consent compliance.
Purchase History
Purchase entitlements and product identifiers are processed to unlock content, verify transactions, and restore purchases.
Ad Interaction
Information about ads viewed, tapped, or completed (e.g., rewarded videos) may be shared with ad networks for measurement and optimization.

Your Choices

How you can control advertising and data

App Tracking Transparency (iOS only)

On iOS 14.5 and later, the app will request your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before using your advertising identifier for cross-app tracking. If you decline, ads will still be shown but they will not be personalized based on cross-app data. The Android app does not use App Tracking Transparency, which is an Apple feature.

Android advertising ID

On Android, you can reset your advertising ID, or delete it so that apps can no longer use it, in Android Settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on your device). You can also opt out of ad personalization there. Ads then remain, but they are not personalized using the advertising ID.

Consent management

In regions where required (EU/EEA and UK), you will see a consent dialog on first launch. You can update your consent preferences at any time from the app's settings screen.

Remove ads

You can purchase an ad-free option within the app. This removes banner, interstitial, and app-open ads. Rewarded video ads may still be available on an opt-in basis if you choose to watch them for in-game bonuses.

Additional Information

What the app does not collect

No account required

Nomad Kitchen Tycoon does not require you to create an account with a name, email, or password. You can play the full game without signing up for anything.

No payment data collected

The app never collects credit card numbers or direct payment details. All payment processing is handled securely by Apple through the App Store (iOS) or by Google through Google Play (Android).

Reset your data

The app includes a reset option in Settings to clear all local progress and start fresh. For questions about data held by third-party analytics or ad services, contact us at the email above.

Contact support

If you email us for support, we receive only the information you include in your message. We use it solely to respond to your request and do not share it with third parties.
